Privacy Policy

JobHunter Career AI

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. About the application

JobHunter Career AI is a personal application used by its owner to research, organize and evaluate employment opportunities.

2. Google user data

The application requests read-only access to Google Sheets through the Google Sheets API.

This access is used to read existing job records, identify duplicate opportunities and support career research.

The application does not use this authorization to modify Google Sheets.

3. Data storage and sharing

Job research records and application data may be stored locally on the owner's computer.

Google user data is not sold to advertisers or used for advertising purposes.

The application is intended for personal use and does not provide external user accounts.

4. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored records may be retained for ongoing career research and can be deleted by the application owner.

Google account access can be revoked through Google Account permissions.

5. Security

OAuth credentials and application data are intended to remain private and are not published on this website.

6. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the application owner through the support email listed on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
